AI Fusion – AI Copilot

AI copilot for Autodesk Fusion. AI Fusion adds a docked chat palette to Fusion that drives your design natively — sketching, modelling, editing features, querying the timeline — through normal conversation.

What gets sent over the network

Your prompts and the tool calls AI Fusion runs on your behalf are sent to fusion.adam.new and forwarded to the model provider you've selected (Anthropic, OpenAI, or Google). Tool calls and their results — feature parameters, entity tokens, screenshots when you take them — travel the same way.

AI Fusion does not read or upload your .f3d / .f3z files. All Fusion API work happens in-process inside Fusion via this add-in; only the chat protocol leaves your machine.

Sign-in uses your existing Autodesk account via Autodesk Platform Services (APS) OAuth. AI Fusion requests the openid and user-profile:read scopes only — no data:* or bucket:*.

Install / upgrade

The simplest path is the installer at https://fusion.adam.new/install. One-liners for each platform:

macOS — Terminal

curl -sSL https://fusion.adam.new/install.sh | bash

Windows — PowerShell

irm https://fusion.adam.new/install.ps1 | iex

After install, fully quit Fusion, reopen it, then in Fusion press Shift+S → Add-Ins tab → find AIFusion → click Run and tick Run on Startup. Fusion ships new add-ins disabled until you click Run once; after that it auto-loads on every launch. If you ever close the AI Fusion palette, reopen it from the Utilities tab → Add-Ins panel → AI Fusion – AI Copilot button (or rerun the add-in from Shift+S).

Re-running the installer upgrades in place.

Uninstall

Quit Fusion, then remove the add-in directory:

  • macOS: rm -rf "$HOME/Library/Application Support/Autodesk/Autodesk Fusion 360/API/AddIns/AIFusion"
  • Windows: delete %APPDATA%\Autodesk\Autodesk Fusion 360\API\AddIns\AIFusion

To stop billing, cancel from https://fusion.adam.new/billing before uninstalling — uninstalling the add-in alone does not cancel a subscription.
